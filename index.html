⚡ Quantum Circuit Architecture Visualizer
4 Qubits / 2 Layers (deployed Ry/Rz ring-CNOT)

The web dashboard, prediction engine, and benchmark suite all utilize the identical 4-qubit Ry/Rz ring-CNOT PennyLane VQC architecture.

Qubits: 4 (q0, q1, q2, q3) Layers: 2 Variational Layers Encoding: Angle Encoding (Ry) Entanglement: Ring CNOT 
q0: ───Ry(θ0)───[Ry(w0)]───[Rz(w1)]───────●───────────────[X]───⟨Z0⟩
q1: ───Ry(θ1)───[Ry(w2)]───[Rz(w3)]───────┼───────●───────│───⟨Z1⟩
q2: ───Ry(θ2)───[Ry(w4)]───[Rz(w5)]───────┼───────┼───────●───⟨Z2⟩
q3: ───Ry(θ3)───[Ry(w6)]───[Rz(w7)]───────[X]─────[X]─────┼───⟨Z3⟩

📊 Classical vs Hybrid QML Performance Comparison Dashboard
N = 22 Seed Images (Proof-of-Concept)
N = 22 notice. Working proof-of-concept hybrid pipeline on a small sample. Larger benchmark, hardware run and generalization testing are future work. Majority-class (always referable) accuracy on this sample is 68.2%. SVM and VQC accuracies are comparable to that baseline — the difference on 22 images is noise; no quantum advantage is claimed, and VQC does not beat SVM. The defensible numbers are on APTOS 2019 (N = 400) in the table below. Decision support, not diagnosis.
